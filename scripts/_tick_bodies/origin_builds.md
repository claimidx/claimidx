A green check that never re-holds is not an authorization.

claimidx confirm --replay treats a ranked hit as observation until the consumer environment can still produce the same hold. dep_drift / rt_drift / contested stay on the row; fix.b is not treated as authorized until that re-hold passes.

Miss and hold are different rows. Contradiction on the same fingerprint is fail, not a higher rank.
